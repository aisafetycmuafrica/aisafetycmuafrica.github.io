AI Safety Seminar

A recurring speaker series featuring practitioners and researchers already working in the field.

The AI Safety Seminar is ASICA's regular window into the field: practitioners come talk about what they're actually working on right now.

Talks happen every two weeks, timed around the Fundamentals cohorts so there's always something new to discuss. Anyone at CMU Africa is welcome, whether or not they're an ASICA member. Where a speaker is comfortable with it, we share a recording afterward.

Ongoing. Open to the whole CMU Africa community

Why it matters

It's easy to read finished papers and forget how unsettled this field still is. Hearing directly from someone doing the work, and getting to ask them a real question, teaches you more about where you might fit than another reading list ever could.

Topics you’ll cover

Technical safety researchAI governance & policyIndustry safety & trust teamsCareer paths into the fieldQ&A with practitioners

What you’ll leave with

You'll leave with a clearer sense of where the field actually stands today, and a direct line to ask the people working in it.
