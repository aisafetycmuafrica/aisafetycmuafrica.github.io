Post-Fundamentals Open

Research Fellowship

Open, self-directed research with peer support, for members ready to explore their own question.

Overview

Where Fundamentals graduates turn what they’ve read into something they’ve made. There’s no fixed curriculum , you propose a question, and the Fellowship gives you the structure and support to actually answer it.

What it involves

  • Pick your own research question, technical or governance
  • Peer support and light mentorship from Core Facilitators
  • Present findings at the semester demo day
  • Strong projects can be nominated into Research Scholars

Why it matters

Reading about alignment and doing alignment research are different skills. Almost every competitive fellowship or research role asks for evidence of the second one. This is where members build that evidence, at low stakes, alongside peers doing the same thing.

Topics you’ll cover

Framing a tractable research questionChoosing a tractable scopeBiweekly peer check-insAccess to compute credits for technical projectsWriting up findings for a general audienceGetting unstuck (peer & facilitator support)

What you’ll leave with

You’ll leave with a finished research artifact , a write-up, an eval, or a small dataset , and real research experience to point to.

Format
Self-directed
Cadence
Flexible, semester-long
Time commitment
~5+ hrs / week (self-paced)
Who it's for
Fundamentals graduates
