AI Alignment Fundamentals

A technical reading cohort on the core alignment literature, in small facilitator-led groups.

Overview

ASICA’s entry point into technical AI safety. Each cohort works through a curated sequence of papers and posts covering how modern AI systems are trained, where that training goes wrong, and what current research is doing about it.

What it involves

  • Weekly facilitator-led discussion of core alignment papers
  • Small groups of 6–10 for real discussion, not lecture
  • Ends with an optional capstone, from a short write-up to a small project
  • Graduates can apply to the Research Fellowship

Why it matters

Most technical safety roles start from the same baseline: can you read a paper, name the problem it’s solving, and reason about where the approach might fail. This program builds exactly that baseline, so members aren’t starting from zero when they apply to fellowships like MATS or SPAR.

Topics you’ll cover

RLHF & reward modelingInterpretabilityRed-teaming & jailbreakingScalable oversightAI controlDeceptive alignment & reward hackingEval design basics

What you’ll leave with

You’ll leave comfortable reading primary alignment papers on your own, with a finished capstone and a sense of which sub-area you want to go deeper on.

Format
6–8 week cohort
Cadence
Weekly, small groups
Time commitment
~3 hrs / week
Who it's for
Technically-minded members, any year
