Research Scholars

Structured, faculty and PhD-mentored research on a scoped project, for the most engaged members.

Research Scholars is a smaller step up from the Fellowship. Instead of an open question, you work on one scoped project defined together with a mentor from day one.

Expect regular one-on-one time with a faculty member or PhD student, and a project aimed at something you could actually submit somewhere: a workshop, a conference, a write-up worth sharing outside CMU Africa.

Launching after this pilot semester

Why it matters

Not every strong Fellowship project needs this level of mentorship to finish. Some do, especially the ones aiming at a genuinely novel result. Scholars exists for those, so the strongest work coming out of ASICA has a real shot at being read outside CMU Africa.

Topics you’ll cover

Working from an existing research agendaRegular 1:1s with a mentorAiming at a workshop or conference submissionPreparing a submission

What you’ll leave with

You'll leave with a mentored research output aimed at an actual submission, rather than a semester project that ends in a drawer.
