Hackathon

An Apart Research-style sprint , a short window to produce a concrete red-team finding or eval.

The Hackathon takes the Research Fellowship's whole arc, picking a question, doing the work, presenting it, and compresses it into a single weekend, using a real model instead of a hypothetical one.

Teams form on the spot. You'll spend the weekend red-teaming, evaluating, or probing a model, then present what you found. Open-weight models and compute are provided, so nobody's blocked on access.

Once a semester. Open to all members

Why it matters

Red-teaming and evaluation work are some of the most learnable-by-doing skills in this field right now. A single weekend is a low-cost way to find out if that kind of work is for you before committing a whole semester to the Fellowship.

Topics you’ll cover

Red-teaming a live modelWriting a short evalPresenting a finding clearly and fastScoring & write-up format

What you’ll leave with

You'll leave with one concrete finding from a single weekend, and maybe the start of a Fellowship project.
