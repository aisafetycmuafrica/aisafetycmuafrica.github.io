AI Governance Fundamentals

The policy counterpart, covering regulation, institutional design, and the governance side of frontier AI.

AI Governance Fundamentals looks at the same problem as the Alignment track, powerful systems moving faster than our ability to manage them, but through policy and institutions instead of code.

The format mirrors Alignment Fundamentals: weekly sessions, small groups, real cases instead of lecture. Discussions cover regulation, how international coordination actually works, and how institutions get built around new technology. The track ends with a policy brief or a short capstone, and graduates can move into the Research Fellowship from here too.

Paused for this pilot semester. Expected to return.

Why it matters

Technical fixes only go as far as the institutions willing to adopt them. This track gives members the vocabulary and context to engage seriously with regulation and coordination, and gives our more technical members a second lens on the same underlying problem.

Topics you’ll cover

Regulation & policy frameworksCompute governanceInternational coordinationModel evaluations & auditsInstitutional designStandards & auditsCase studies in AI regulation

What you’ll leave with

You'll come out with a working map of the governance landscape, a finished policy brief, and a better sense of where you could actually contribute.

Format
6 to 8 week cohort
Cadence

Time commitment
~3 hrs / week
Who it's for
Policy-minded members, any year
