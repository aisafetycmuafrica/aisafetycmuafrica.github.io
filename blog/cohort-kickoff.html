Cohort kickoff: 35 members start AI Alignment Fundamentals

ASICA Team · 3 October 2026

Our first AI Alignment Fundamentals cohort is underway. Here is what ASICA is, why this work matters now, and the road ahead across seven units.

At a glance
35 Cohort members joined
7 Units in the fundamentals arc
3 Oct Kickoff, Fall 2026

What ASICA is

AI capabilities are advancing faster than our ability to evaluate, align, or govern them.

ASICA, the AI Safety Initiative at CMU-Africa, brings together researchers, engineers, and policy thinkers to build the technical and governance tools that let us evaluate systems before deployment, not after. The AI Alignment Fundamentals cohort is where most members start: a structured grounding in alignment that opens into a research pipeline as you go further.

Why safety now

Why it matters

Faster than predicted

Systems that were research curiosities a few years ago can now write code, conduct research, and reason through complex problems. The gap between today's AI and systems that outperform humans at most cognitive tasks may be years, not decades.

The core difficulty

We do not yet know how to reliably specify what we want a highly capable system to do, verify that it is doing it, or correct it if it is not. These problems need solving before the stakes get highest, not after.
